# 💫 About Me

## 👋 About Me

Hi, I’m Abrar — a Medical Engineering master’s student in Germany, specializing in **Medical Image and Data Processing**.

My academic focus is on computational methods for biomedical data, medical imaging workflows, signal/data analysis, and building intelligent systems that support healthcare and engineering applications.

Alongside my studies, I work as a **Software Infrastructure Working Student at Siemens Energy**, where I support cloud-based digital platforms, data pipelines, CI/CD workflows, AWS infrastructure, and engineering data automation.

Previously, I worked on IoT and digital twin platforms at Tata Elxsi, building Python-based microservices, REST APIs, telemetry pipelines, and event-driven backend systems.

I’m especially interested in the intersection of **Medical Imaging, Data Processing, Cloud Infrastructure, Applied AI, and Reliable Software Systems**.
